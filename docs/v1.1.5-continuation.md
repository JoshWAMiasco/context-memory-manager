What was built:

- Terminals run in a detached `terminal-host.js` process (`server/terminal/host-process.ts`, `host-client.ts`,
  `host-protocol.ts`). CMM talks to it over a socket in `<dataDir>/terminal-hosts/`. When CMM disconnects (it closed,
  restarted, was updated or crashed), the host ends every terminal that isn't a job and keeps the jobs. CMM finds them
  again when it starts. A host left by an older CMM version keeps serving its jobs. New terminals go to a host of the
  current version.
- `TerminalSummary.isJob`. `PATCH /api/terminals/<id> { isJob }` turns a terminal into a job or back.
  `POST /api/terminals` takes `isJob`.
- UI: **Run as a job** on terminal title bars and the docked column. A `/jobs` page with cards showing when the job
  started, how long it has run and where. `?id=` opens one job across the whole page, with Open in editor, Make a
  terminal and Discard. Jobs also show as cards on the dashboard and in the navigation. Jobs stay out of the
  terminal dock unless opened in the editor.
- `deploy/linux/cmm.service`: `KillMode=process`, so stopping or restarting the service doesn't kill the jobs.
  `uninstall.sh` ends them. In server mode, shells get `TMPDIR=/var/lib/cmm/tmp`, because the service's private
  `/tmp` is deleted when it stops.
- Tests: unit (`tests/server/terminal/attach.test.ts`, `tests/lib/terminal.test.ts`), e2e (`e2e/jobs.web.spec.ts`),
  and scripted restart and version-upgrade runs against the built server.
